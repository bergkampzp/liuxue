Entry requirements for students from China

The University of Sheffield accepts a number of different qualifications from China. Some of the most common are listed below. If your qualification is not listed here, please contact us.

On

English language requirements

All applicants are required to have an internationally recognised English language qualification such as IELTS. The minimum score that is accepted for entry is IELTS 6.0 or equivalent for undergraduate study and 6.5 for postgraduate study. Some departments may require a higher score.

English language entry requirements - find out more

English Language Teaching Centre (ELTC)

For those applicants who do not currently have the necessary English language requirements, the University of Sheffield is able to offer a number of English language courses at our English Language Teaching Centre.

Undergraduate Programmes e.g. BSc, BA, BEng/ MEng

A variety of qualifications can be considered for direct admission to undergraduate bachelor degree programmes. All applications for undergraduate programmes should be made through UCAS.

Visit the UCAS website for more information

Gaokao

We accept the Gaokao for direct entry to our undergraduate programmes.

For courses with A Level grades of AAA or above we require an overall score equivalent to at least 75% of the total marks available. For courses with entry requirements of AAB - ABB we require an equivalent score of at least 70% and for courses with entry requirements of BBB - BBC we require an equivalent score of at least 65%

For courses with individual A Level subject requirements, you must achieve the required score in the specified subject (typically Maths and/or a science subject) taken as part of the Gaokao.

  • A Level grade A – a score equivalent to 75% of the total marks in the Gaokao subject
  • A Level grade B – a score equivalent to 70% of the total marks in the Gaokao subject
  • A Level grade C – a score equivalent to 65% of the total marks in the Gaokao subject

Chinese Senior High School Certificate (高中毕业证)

Applicants who have successfully completed the 3 year Chinese Senior High School Certificate (高中毕业证) will need to complete a recognised Foundation programme (预科) to be considered for entry into the first year of undergraduate programmes. Please contact the China Team to check if the Foundation Programme (预科) that you are considering is acceptable for entry.

Undergraduate pathway programmes at the University of Sheffield International College

The University of Sheffield International College provides an International Foundation Year programme and an International Year One programme specifically for international students who want to progress to the University of Sheffield. Upon successful completion, students can progress to an undergraduate degree.

Students can choose to study an International Foundation Year in the following subjects:

  • International Foundation Year in Business, Social Sciences and Humanities
  • International Foundation Year in Science and Engineering

Students can choose to study an International Year One in the following subjects:

  • International Year One in Business Management and Accounting
  • International Year One in Economics 

Prospective students from China can visit the University of Sheffield International College Chinese website for more information.

Northern Consortium UK (NCUK)

The University is a member of the Northern Consortium (NCUK) which offers an International Foundation Year (IFY) and International Year One (IYOne) across China.

Students who have completed the IFY programme and have obtained the required grades will be considered for admission into the first year of undergraduate programmes. Students who complete the IYOne, upon successful completion, can apply to enter the second year of a wide range of degree courses.

Visit the NCUK website to find out more.

First Year University Degree Students

Applicants who have successfully completed the first year of a Chinese university bachelor degree (standard four-year course) will be considered for entry into the first year of undergraduate programmes.

Chinese Three Year University Diploma (大专)

Applicants holding a three year university diploma will be considered for direct entry into the second year of University of Sheffield undergraduate courses.

A Level

Applicants with a minimum of 3 A levels will be considered for direct admission to undergraduate programmes.

International Baccalaureate

International Baccalaureate diploma holders will be considered for direct admission to undergraduate programmes.

Aptitude Scholastic Test (AST)

Applicants who have taken three ASTs and achieved marks of 200 in each subject (including Maths and English), can be considered for first year entry to our courses within the Faculty of Arts & Humanities and Faculty of Social Sciences (excluding BSc Economics and BSc Economics dual courses). Applicants must also be completing, or have completed the 3 year Chinese Senior High School Certificate with 75% overall. Entry to other courses will not be considered.

Chinese University Degree Holders

You will usually need a 4 year bachelors degree* from a recognised university in a relevant subject for entry.  Most postgraduate courses require a UK 2:1 (or equivalent) for entry, but some courses may consider 2:2 grades.  Please see our course finder for specific course requirements. 

The score required will depend on the university you have attended and the course to which you have applied.

Postgraduate Progression Routes

If you complete a single award Bachelor degree consisting of 3 years study in China with a 1 year UK top up(3+1), we will assess your application based on your UK attainment. 

If your degree is via the 2+2 route, where you study your final 2 years at a UK university, we will assess your application based on your UK attainment.

If you are studying a 3+2 degree programme, where you complete a junior college diploma(Zhuanke) and then a 2 year(Benke 本科) top-up to a Bachelor, please use table 1 to find your required grade based on the ranking of the top-up institution. The 2 year Benke must be via the regular higher education route.

If your degree is a collaboration but dual award, where you will be awarded a bachelor degree from two institutions, then your qualification will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis. Please provide any relevant documentation detailing the format of the collaboration. 

Postgraduate pathways at the University of Sheffield International College

The University of Sheffield International College provides Pre-Masters programmes specifically for international students who want to progress to the University of Sheffield. Upon successful completion, students can progress to a postgraduate degree.

Students can choose to study an Pre-Masters in the following subjects:

  • Pre-Masters in Business, Social Sciences and Humanities
  • Pre-Masters in Science and Engineering

Prospective students from China can visit the University of Sheffield International College Chinese website for more information.

International pathways at the Northern Consortium UK (NCUK)

The University is a member of the Northern Consortium (NCUK) which offers pre-masters courses in centres across China. 

NCUK  pre-masters courses  can only be considered to meet English language requirements .  An undergraduate degree qualification is still required to meet academic entry requirements for post graduate courses. 

Visit the NCUK website to find out more.

For Entry to Postgraduate Research Programmes e.g. MPhil, PhD, PhD with Integrated Masters

With around 2,000 postgraduate research students from over 100 different countries, Sheffield is one of the leading centres for research training in the UK.

Chinese Masters Degree Holders

You will usually need a bachelors degree AND a masters degree from a recognised university in a relevant subject for entry. Some courses may consider a bachelors degree on its own if it is combined with relevant research experience. Please see our departmental web pages for specific course requirements.
